CODACON Inc. ("CODACON," "we," "us") is a Quebec-registered corporation. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information.

### Information we collect

**Website visitors:** This website does not use cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party scripts. We do not collect personal information through the website unless you contact us directly.

**Email correspondence:** When you contact us by email, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, organization, and the substance of your inquiry. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and, if applicable, to scope a potential engagement.

**Client engagements:** During the course of professional engagements, we may process personal information as required to deliver the contracted services. The terms of such processing are governed by the engagement agreement.

### How we use information

We use personal information only for the purpose for which it was provided:

- Responding to inquiries
- Scoping and delivering professional engagements
- Complying with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use personal information for automated decision-making or profiling.

### Data retention

We retain correspondence and engagement records for the duration necessary to fulfill the purpose for which information was collected, and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal and professional obligations.

### Data protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Specific measures depend on the nature of the information and are proportionate to the sensitivity of the data.

### Your rights

Under Quebec privacy legislation (Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as amended by Law 25) and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

- Access personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
- Withdraw consent to processing, where consent is the basis for processing
- File a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec
