## Executive coaching: secure software delivery in the age of AI

Your engineers are already using AI to write code. Your board is already asking what that means for risk. This program gives you, personally, the judgment to lead the answer rather than delegate it.

### Who it is for

- CTOs and VPs of Engineering accountable for delivery and for what ships
- CISOs whose security program was designed before AI coding tools existed
- Founders of technical companies preparing for enterprise customers, audits or a board

### What you leave with

- A clear position on where AI belongs in your SDLC, where it does not, and how you will know
- A threat model for your own delivery pipeline, in your words, that you can defend in front of a board
- The questions to ask vendors, auditors and your own team, and what a good answer sounds like
- A 90-day operating plan your team can execute without you in the room

### Format

Private sessions, typically six over twelve weeks, by video or in person in Montreal. On-site delivery at your location is available. Between sessions: short written reviews of the decisions you are actually facing. Confidential.
