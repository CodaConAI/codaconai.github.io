<h2 id="build">Build — AI Systems and Automation</h2>

**Production AI capability your team can own.**

We design, build, and harden AI-driven systems — from automated workflows to decision-support tools — so they perform reliably, fail safely, and remain under your team's control.

- AI-driven workflow design and implementation
- LLM integration architecture (retrieval, agents, evaluation pipelines)
- AI-augmented software development lifecycle (AI-driven SDLC)
- Data pipeline security and integrity
- Production readiness assessment for AI systems

**Ideal entry point:** You have an AI pilot that works in demo but needs production-grade architecture, security review, and a handoff plan.

---

<h2 id="secure">Secure — AI and Cloud Security</h2>

**Security grounded in architecture, not compliance theatre.**

We secure AI systems and cloud infrastructure from design through operation. Threat modeling, hardening, detection, and response — built so your team maintains it after we leave.

- Cloud security architecture on AWS and Azure (IAM, network segmentation, encryption)
- AI system threat modeling and adversarial testing
- Incident response capability — detection, triage, playbook development, tabletop exercises
- CI/CD pipeline hardening and DevSecOps integration
- Infrastructure as code security review

**Ideal entry point:** You need a security architecture review of your cloud environment or AI system before a compliance milestone or product launch.

---

<h2 id="govern">Govern — Governance and Resilience</h2>

**Governance that engineers can execute and leadership can defend.**

We help organizations build AI governance and cloud governance frameworks grounded in what actually runs — policy as code, testable controls, and accountability structures that survive contact with production.

- AI governance framework development
- Policy as code for cloud and AI systems
- Regulatory readiness and compliance posture (SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST CSF)
- Board-level AI risk briefings and security posture reporting
- Third-party AI and vendor risk evaluation
- Capability transfer — training and coaching on AI security, DevSecOps, and cloud governance as a component of engagements

**Ideal entry point:** Your organization adopted AI faster than your governance framework and you need a practical path from current state to auditable controls.
